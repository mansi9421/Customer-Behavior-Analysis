# Customer Behavior Dashboard

## Recommended Structure and Order

### 1. Project Title / Headline

**Customer Behavior Analytics: Sales, Revenue & Subscription Insights Dashboard**

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer purchasing behavior, subscription status, product category performance, revenue, sales, and customer demographics.

### 2. Short Description / Purpose

The Customer Behavior Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence solution developed to understand customer purchasing patterns and analyze sales performance across different product categories and age groups.

The dashboard provides insights into customer demographics, subscription preferences, average purchase amount, customer review ratings, revenue distribution, and sales trends. It helps businesses understand customer behavior and make data-driven decisions to improve customer engagement, marketing strategies, and sales performance.

### 3. Tech Stack

The project uses the following tools and technologies:

- **Power BI Desktop:** Used to create interactive dashboards, KPI cards, charts, and reports.
- **Power Query:** Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Used to calculate KPIs, averages, revenue, and other customer metrics.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Used for data inspection and preparation, if applicable.

### 4. Dataset Overview

The dashboard analyzes approximately 3.9K customers and presents customer behavior across several dimensions, including:

- Customer subscription status
- Gender
- Product categories
- Shipping types
- Customer age groups
- Purchase amounts
- Customer review ratings

### 5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard highlights three primary KPIs:

1. **Number of Customers:** 3.9K
2. **Average Purchase Amount:** $59.76
3. **Average Review Rating:** 3.75

### 6. Dashboard Visualizations

**A. Subscription Status Analysis**
- Donut chart showing the percentage of customers by subscription status.
- Displays 73% non-subscribers and 27% subscribers.
- Helps understand customer subscription adoption.

**B. Revenue by Category**
- Column chart comparing revenue across Clothing, Accessories, Footwear, and Outerwear.
- Helps identify categories generating the highest revenue.

**C. Sales by Category**
- Column chart comparing sales across product categories.
- Highlights the relative sales performance of different categories.

**D. Revenue by Age Group**
- Horizontal bar chart comparing revenue among Young Adult, Middle-aged, Adult, and Senior customers.
- Helps identify age groups contributing most to revenue.

**E. Sales by Age Group**
- Horizontal bar chart comparing sales across customer age groups.
- Supports demographic-based sales analysis.

**F. Interactive Filters**
- Subscription status
- Gender
- Product category
- Shipping type

These filters allow users to explore customer behavior across different customer segments.

### 7. Key Business Insights

- The dashboard represents approximately 3.9K customers.
- The average purchase amount is $59.76, providing a baseline for evaluating customer spending.
- The average review rating is 3.75, which can help assess customer feedback and satisfaction trends.
- Approximately 27% of customers have a subscription, while 73% do not.
- Clothing appears to be the leading category in both revenue and sales among the categories displayed.
- The Young Adult group leads the displayed age-group revenue comparison, while age groups show differences in their sales contributions.

### 8. Business Recommendations

- Explore targeted campaigns to encourage non-subscribers to subscribe.
- Analyze the reasons behind customer ratings to identify opportunities to improve customer experience.
- Prioritize high-performing categories while evaluating opportunities to improve sales in lower-performing categories.
- Develop age-group-specific marketing strategies based on purchasing patterns.
- Use the interactive filters to compare customer segments and identify opportunities for personalized promotions.

### 9. Business Impact

The dashboard consolidates customer, sales, revenue, and subscription metrics into a single interactive report. It helps business stakeholders understand customer segments, identify high-performing product categories, and make informed decisions about marketing, customer engagement, and sales strategies.
